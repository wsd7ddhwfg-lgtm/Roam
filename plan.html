1 · Project overview

Roam is a peer-to-peer vehicle rental marketplace for Wellington — open to anyone to rent or list. Renters borrow cars and campervans from locals; hosts earn from idle vehicles. Wellington is the launch market; the brand itself doesn't carry the city name.

Roam Pass is a future paid perk layer (DoorDash Pass model). Non-Pass users can still use Roam normally; Pass-holders get extras like premium vehicle access, host bonuses, and member events. The Pass is not built yet — its mention on the landing page is purely a demand signal (a single checkbox in the sign-up form).

This landing page is a demand test. It validates two things at once:

  • Will Wellingtonians sign up to use Roam (as renters, hosts, or both)?
  • How many of those signups also tick the Pass interest checkbox?

5 · "How It Works" — the new section

Two-column visual journey. The icons in each column tell the whole story before anyone reads a word. Yellow = renters' path. Mint = hosts' path.

Section header

Eyebrow: HOW IT WORKS
Headline (Anton, ~72px): "FIVE STEPS. BOTH WAYS."
Sub: Renting or hosting — here's the (very short) path.

For renters → steering wheel

01user-plus
Create your account. Quick sign-up, verified driver, you're in.
02car
Pick your ride. Browse cars and campervans from your suburb.
03timer
Hours or days. A 3-hour grocery run, or a 10-day campervan saga.
04map-pin
Pick up or delivered. Grab it nearby, or have it dropped to your door.
05wind
Hit the road. Cover comes with the trip. Just drive.

For hosts → payday

01user-plus
Create your account. Quick sign-up, list your vehicle in minutes.
02shield-check
Trust survey. A short check so you know your car's in safe hands.
03calendar
Set your hours. Choose when your vehicle's available — and when it's not.
04bell
Quick accept. Get pinged the moment a booking comes in. Approve in a tap.
05wallet
Cash on collect. Paid as soon as the keys hand over.

Why this works: No flowchart, no arrows. Just a single color cue (yellow vs mint) telling the brain which path is which, and an icon vocabulary that does the heavy lifting. The 5 icons in each column tell the journey without anyone needing to read.

6 · Copy voice and claim rules

The page reads as confident and exciting, but every sentence is checked for hidden promises. Founding signups are about access and identity, not concrete service guarantees.

✓ In scope (intent + identity)

  • "Built local, by locals."
  • "Wellington's own peer-to-peer marketplace."
  • "Cover comes with the trip." (intent statement)
  • "Roam Pass is on the way."
  • "Founding members get in first."
  • "Wellington beta is on the way."

✗ Out of scope (specific promises)

  • Specific fee percentages ("10%")
  • Specific payout timing ("every Tuesday")
  • Specific launch dates ("Q1 2027")
  • Specific waitlist counts ("402 signed up")
  • Insurance terms ("comprehensive cover with $X excess")
  • Service guarantees ("24/7 support", "instant approval")

7 · What's next — Phase B: Next.js build

Once D5 is locked, the landing page gets built as a real responsive site. Stack:

  • Next.js 14 + Tailwind CSS — single-page, fully responsive
  • Tally embedded forms — signups route to a Google Sheet
  • Vercel hosting on the free tier
  • Domain — `.co.nz` to be secured (recommend `roamwellington.co.nz` or `roamnz.co.nz`)
